## Gönderi 1 – S1, S2, S3 ne demek? (Salı/Çarşamba sabahı)
Choosing the right safety shoe starts with the class on the label. Under EN ISO 20345, the most common ones are:

🔹 S1 – toe protection (200 J), closed heel, antistatic, energy-absorbing heel
🔹 S2 – S1 + water-resistant upper
🔹 S3 – S2 + puncture-resistant midsole + cleated outsole

Rule of thumb: dry indoor work → S1/S1P. Outdoor sites, wet floors or nails → S3.

Buying for a team? Tell us the work environment and we'll suggest the right class.

## Gönderi 4 – Numune neden önemli
Before ordering safety shoes in volume, always test a sample pair on site for one or two weeks.

What to check:
• Fit and comfort after a full shift
• Sole grip on your actual floors
• Toe cap and heel after daily wear
• Markings inside the tongue: standard, class (S1P/S3), size, date

A good supplier is happy to send samples. If they hesitate, that's a signal.

#PPE #SafetyFootwear #Procurement #HSE

## Gönderi 5 – Etiketi nasıl okursunuz
How to read a safety shoe label in 10 seconds 👟

EN ISO 20345:2022 → the standard
S3 → the protection class
SRC / SR → slip resistance tested
HRO → heat-resistant outsole (up to 300°C contact)
CI / HI → cold / heat insulation

If the label is missing or unclear, ask for the certificate from a notified body.

#SafetyFootwear #PPE #HSE #Compliance

## Gönderi 6 – Kompozit mi çelik mi
Steel or composite toe caps? Both give 200 J protection under EN ISO 20345.

Composite: lighter, doesn't conduct heat or cold, metal-detector friendly
Steel: slimmer profile, usually lower cost

For hot climates and airports/plants with metal detectors, composite often wins.
