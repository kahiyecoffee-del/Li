# Satış Temsilciliği Sözleşmesi – Taslak

> **Önemli:** Bu bir başlangıç taslağıdır, hukuki tavsiye değildir. İmzalamadan önce uluslararası ticaret hukukunda deneyimli bir avukata kontrol ettir. İlk kontrolden sonra şablon olarak tekrar tekrar kullanabilirsin.
>
> **Nasıl kullanılır:** Üretici temsilcilik teklifini (A5 mesajı) kabul edince bu metni doldurup gönder. Köşeli parantezleri doldur. Üretici değişiklik isteyebilir; kırmızı çizgilerin aşağıda.

**Pazarlıkta asla vazgeçme (kırmızı çizgiler):**
1. Tekrar eden siparişlere komisyon (madde 4.2)
2. Sözleşme bittikten sonraki koruma süresi (madde 4.3)
3. Atlatma yasağı (madde 6)
4. Fatura kopyası görme hakkı (madde 5)

**Pazarlıkta esneyebilirsin:** Komisyon oranı, bölge genişliği, münhasırlık (başta münhasır olmayan kabul edilebilir).

### 2. Role of the Representative
2.1 The Representative shall promote the Products in the Territory, solicit orders from clients and forward such orders to the Principal. The Representative shall act as the primary point of contact between the Principal and clients obtained by the Representative.
2.1a **Orders:** The Representative shall forward each order in writing using the Principal's current price list and terms. Every order is subject to written acceptance by the Principal. The Principal is responsible for order confirmation, production, invoicing the client, collecting payment, shipping, export documentation and after-sales service.
2.1b **Communication:** The Principal shall not contact clients obtained by the Representative except through, or with the Representative in copy on, all communications, unless the Representative agrees otherwise in writing.
2.2 The Representative has no authority to enter into contracts, accept orders, set prices or make commitments on behalf of the Principal.
2.3 The Representative is an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment, partnership or joint-venture relationship.
2.4 The Representative bears its own costs unless otherwise agreed in writing.

### 3. Client Registration
3.1 Before introducing a prospect, the Representative shall register it with the Principal by e-mail, stating the company name and country.
3.2 The Principal shall, within three (3) business days, notify the Representative if the prospect is already an existing client of the Principal (having placed an order within the previous 24 months). Without such notice, the prospect becomes a "Registered Client".
3.3 Any client whose order is forwarded by the Representative, or who places an order with the Principal following contact by the Representative, is a Registered Client, whether or not formally registered.

### 4. Commission
4.1 The Principal shall pay the Representative a commission of **[5]%** of the net invoice value (excluding freight, insurance and taxes) of all orders placed by Registered Clients.
4.2 **Repeat orders:** Commission is due on all orders from Registered Clients during the term of this Agreement, including repeat orders and orders placed through affiliates, subsidiaries, agents or nominees of the Registered Client.
4.3 **Post-termination protection:** Commission remains due on all orders placed by Registered Clients for **twenty-four (24) months** after expiry or termination of this Agreement, for any reason.
4.4 **Payment:** Commission is payable in USD within ten (10) days of the Principal's receipt of each payment from the Registered Client, by bank transfer to the account specified by the Representative, against the Representative's invoice. Bank charges on the Principal's side are borne by the Principal.
4.5 Commission is not payable on amounts the Registered Client never pays, provided the Principal has made reasonable efforts to collect.

### 5. Information and Reporting
5.1 The Principal shall send the Representative a copy of each commercial invoice issued to a Registered Client within seven (7) days of issue, and notify the Representative of each payment received.
5.2 Upon reasonable request, no more than once per calendar year, the Principal shall provide a statement of all sales to Registered Clients, certified by an authorised officer.

### 6. Non-Circumvention
6.1 The Principal shall not, directly or indirectly (including through affiliates or third parties), solicit or conclude business with any Registered Client in a way intended to avoid paying commission under this Agreement.
6.2 Any such business is subject to commission under Clause 4 as if it were concluded through the Representative.

### 7. Confidentiality
Each party shall keep confidential all non-public information received from the other, including client identities, prices and terms, during the term and for three (3) years thereafter.

### 8. Compliance
8.1 Both parties shall comply with applicable anti-bribery, anti-corruption, export control and sanctions laws (including those of the UN, EU, UK and US).
8.2 Neither party shall be required to undertake any transaction involving sanctioned persons or countries, or controlled military items.
8.3 The Principal warrants that the Products hold the certifications stated in Annex A and is solely responsible for product quality, conformity, delivery and product liability.

### 9. Term and Termination

9.2 Either party may terminate immediately by written notice if the other materially breaches this Agreement and fails to remedy the breach within thirty (30) days of notice.
9.3 Clauses 4, 5, 6, 7 and 10 survive termination.
