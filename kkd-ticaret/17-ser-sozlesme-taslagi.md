**[Eray Burak Sözer / sole proprietorship trade name]**, [address], Türkiye, trading as Bridgeline Global Sourcing (the "Representative").

### 1. Appointment
1.1 The Principal appoints the Representative as its **non-exclusive** independent sales representative for the Products in the Territory.
1.2 **Products:** industrial safety footwear under the brands Rigel and Yepa listed in the Principal's current export price list (Annex A), excluding the TL-priced Eco series. **Military, police and defence products are excluded.**
1.3 **Territory:** the Gulf countries, Iraq, the Caucasus, Central Asia, the Balkans and Africa, excluding any country or person subject to UN, EU, UK or US sanctions.

### 2. Role of the Representative
2.1 The Representative promotes the Products, solicits quotation requests and orders and forwards them in writing to the Principal. Every order is subject to written acceptance by the Principal.
2.2 The Principal handles order confirmation, production, invoicing, collection of payment, export documentation, shipping and after-sales service.
2.3 The Representative has no authority to bind the Principal. It is an independent contractor and bears its own costs.
2.4 **Communication:** the Principal shall not contact Registered Clients except through, or with the Representative in copy on, all communications, unless the Representative agrees in writing.

### 3. Prices and Offers
3.1 The Principal's net unit prices are those in Annex A (EUR, EXW Istanbul). The Principal shall notify any change in writing at least thirty (30) days in advance; accepted orders and offers made in writing and valid at that time remain unchanged.
3.2 The Representative offers the Registered Client a price at its discretion, which may include its commission on top of the Principal's net price (the "Client Price"). The Principal shall invoice the Registered Client at the Client Price stated in the accepted order.
3.3 Volume discounts for larger projects are agreed with the Principal in writing before the offer is made.
3.4 Export payment terms are those of the Principal (currently 40% with order confirmation and 60% before shipment by bank transfer, 100% before shipment for stock items), communicated to the client in the offer.

### 4. Client Registration
4.1 Before forwarding a request from a prospect, the Representative registers it by e-mail with company name and country. Buyer identities are disclosed to the Principal only through this registration.
4.2 Within three (3) business days, the Principal informs the Representative if the prospect is an existing client (ordered within the previous 24 months). Otherwise it becomes a "Registered Client".
4.3 Any client whose order is forwarded by the Representative, or who orders following the Representative's contact, is a Registered Client.

### 5. Commission
5.1 The Representative's commission on each order is the difference between the Client Price and the Principal's net price for the same goods, as stated in the accepted order (the "Commission").
5.2 **Repeat orders:** Commission is due on all orders from Registered Clients, including repeat orders and orders placed through their affiliates or nominees, during the term and for **twenty-four (24) months** after expiry or termination for any reason.
5.3 **Payment:** the Principal pays the Commission in EUR (or USD at the Principal's choice at the day's rate) within ten (10) days of receipt of each client payment (including the advance payment), against the Representative's invoice, by bank transfer; the Principal bears its own bank charges.
5.4 No Commission is due on amounts the client never pays, provided the Principal has made reasonable efforts to collect and has informed the Representative in writing.

### 6. Information
6.1 The Principal sends the Representative a copy of each invoice to a Registered Client within seven (7) days and confirms each payment received (bank confirmation or statement extract) within five (5) days.
6.2 Once a year, on request, the Principal provides a statement of sales to Registered Clients.

### 7. Non-Circumvention
The Principal shall not, directly or through affiliates or third parties, solicit or conclude business with a Registered Client to avoid paying Commission. Such business remains subject to Clause 5.

### 8. Product Compliance
8.1 The Principal warrants that the Products carry valid EU type examination certificates and declarations of conformity under Regulation (EU) 2016/425 and EN ISO 20345, and shall provide copies per model within ten (10) days of request (e.g. via a shared link). Annex B lists the certificates provided.
8.2 The Principal is solely responsible for quality, conformity, delivery and product liability. Free samples for serious requests are provided as agreed in writing (preparation time up to ten (10) days).

### 9. Confidentiality and Compliance
9.1 Each party keeps non-public information (client identities, prices, terms) confidential during the term and for three (3) years afterwards.
9.2 Both parties comply with anti-bribery, export control and sanctions laws. Neither is required to transact with sanctioned persons or countries or in military items.

### 10. Term and Termination
10.1 Effective on signature, for one (1) year, renewing automatically for one-year periods unless either party gives sixty (60) days' written notice.
10.2 Either party may terminate by written notice if the other materially breaches and fails to remedy within thirty (30) days.
10.3 Clauses 5, 6, 7, 9 and 11 survive termination.

### 11. Governing Law, Disputes, General
11.1 Laws of the Republic of Türkiye. Disputes: thirty (30) days of good-faith negotiation, then ISTAC arbitration in Istanbul, in Turkish or English, sole arbitrator.
11.2 This Agreement is the entire agreement; changes in writing. Notices by e-mail. It may be signed electronically.
11.3 The Representative may perform this Agreement and issue Commission invoices through a sole proprietorship or company registered in its name and shall notify the registered trade name and tax number before the first invoice; this is not an assignment and Registered Clients remain unchanged.
