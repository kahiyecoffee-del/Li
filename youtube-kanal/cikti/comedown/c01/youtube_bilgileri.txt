In court-ordered group therapy, Mikey tells the story of the night he and his best friend Tuna lost something that wasn't theirs (to a goose) and had until sunrise to pay back $612. A 2 a.m. charity car wash, a pawn shop run by the worst neighbor in town, an emotional extended warranty sold to a cop, and a grandpa who scams them back.

THE COMEDOWN is an original adult animated dark comedy about two 19-year-old losers, bad decisions and the people who keep loving them anyway. New episode every night.
